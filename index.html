Requirement overview

You must complete all three of the following at a minimum of 5 words per minute (adjustable up to 25 wpm in Settings):

  1. Carry on a 5-minute conversation in Morse code.
  2. Correctly copy a 2-minute message sent in Morse code.
  3. Send a 25-word written passage in Morse code.

This app is a self-practice / self-test tool. Requirements #2 and #3 are auto-graded here for practice purposes. Requirement #1 (conversation) is timed and logged, but — like most BSA advancement — a unit leader or merit badge counselor should observe or review the transcript to confirm proficiency before the strip is awarded.

1. Five-Minute Conversation

The app will play short lines in Morse code — the text is never shown, so you have to copy it by ear, just like a real contact. Use the Notes box to write down what you copy, then key your own reply using the button below or the spacebar (press and hold for dots/dashes, like a telegraph key — you'll hear a tone while it's held down). The session runs for 5 minutes. Send at whatever speed feels natural; the app figures out your dots and dashes on its own.

2. Two-Minute Copy Test

A historical Scouting passage will be sent in Morse code (Robert Baden-Powell's writings / Scouting for Boys). As you hear each letter, translate it in your head and type the plain-English word directly into the box below — that's the actual skill this requirement tests. You can pause if you need a moment, and there's no separate "notes" step: what you type in the box is what gets graded.
